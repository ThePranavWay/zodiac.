Mission Briefing

Case Z–068

Investigator,

On 20 December 1968, the first confirmed attack took place on Lake Herman Road, near Benicia, California. Over the next ten months, three more confirmed attacks followed across Northern California — and a trail of letters and coded messages began arriving at Bay Area newspapers.

ESTABLISHED RECORD

  • Four confirmed attacks: Lake Herman Road, Blue Rock Springs, Lake Berryessa, and Presidio Heights
  • Seven people were attacked. Five died; two survived
  • Letters and coded messages were sent to Bay Area newspapers
  • Four cryptograms: Z408 and Z340 solved; Z13 and Z32 remain unsolved
  • The subject signed with a cross-circle symbol — ⊕
  • Identity has never been conclusively established

The subject claimed more than investigators could ever confirm. Separate the claim from the evidence — a claim is not a verified fact.

“This is the Zodiac speaking.”

Decades later, the case has never been conclusively resolved. The files, the ciphers, and the questions remain.

Your mission: Uncover what really happened in California between 1968 and 1969.

Note: Evidence is scattered across multiple archived sites and documents. Follow the clues. Cross-reference everything. The truth reveals itself only through investigation.
